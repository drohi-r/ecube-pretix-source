About Us

Ecube Entertainment Ltd.

Who We Are

Ecube Entertainment Ltd. is a Dhaka-based company specializing in event production, entertainment technology, and digital ticketing. Since 2018, we have been creating memorable experiences through live events, concerts, and entertainment productions across Bangladesh.

Our ticketing platform at tickets.ecube-entertainment.com provides a seamless, secure way for audiences to discover and purchase tickets for events. We combine modern technology with a deep understanding of the local entertainment landscape to deliver reliable ticketing services for both event organizers and attendees.

What We Do

  • Event Production. We plan, produce, and execute live events ranging from concerts and festivals to corporate gatherings and cultural programs.
  • Digital Ticketing. Our platform provides secure online ticket sales with instant digital delivery, powered by SSLCommerz for safe payment processing.
  • Entertainment Technology. We build and operate the technical infrastructure that powers modern event experiences, from ticketing systems to on-site technology.
  • Event Management Services. We offer end-to-end event management for organizers who need professional support in bringing their vision to life.
